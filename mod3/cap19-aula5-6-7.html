Aula 06 – background-attachment e shorthand





body {

background-image: url(imagens/wallpaper003.jpg);

background-attachment: fixed;

}

shorthand - background sequencia

color > image > position > / > size > repeat > attachment

background: url(imagens/wallpaper003.jpg) center center / cover no-repeat fixed;
